Train your mind

In the healing method you cycle the images in your head, not on a screen. These levels take the images away step by step, until you can run through your whole list with your eyes closed. Work through them in order, over several days if you need to.

How the method works
  1. Write a list of 20 or more things you want. They can be big or small.
  2. Pick an image that stands for each one, as if it has already happened.
  3. Memorise the list, then cycle through the images quickly, so fast you can't dwell on any single one.
  4. Keep it light and relaxed. Don't force it. Practise daily, a few minutes at a time.
  5. Change your list as goals are met or new ones come up.
